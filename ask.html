Common Learner Inquiries

What is SEBI?

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is the statutory regulator for securities and commodity markets, protecting investor interests and enforcing market regulations.

What does NIFTY 50 represent?

NIFTY 50 is the benchmark index of the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE), tracking the 50 largest and most actively traded companies across various sectors.

What is a Bull vs. Bear market?

A Bull market describes sustained market rises and optimistic investor sentiment. A Bear market describes a downturn with prolonged price declines (typically 20%+).
