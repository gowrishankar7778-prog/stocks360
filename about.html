About Stocks 360

Stocks 360 is an educational platform created to help people understand the stock market in a simple, clear, and inclusive way. We are passionate about making financial education accessible to everyone, including learners with visual impairments or reading difficulties.

Our Focus & Core Mission

Financial news and analysis can often feel intimidating with complicated terminology and visual-heavy charts. Stocks 360 is built with modern web accessibility principles, featuring:

  • Audio-Pitch Speech Synthesis: Distinct rising audio tones for positive stocks and lower tones for declining stocks.
  • High-Contrast Mode: WCAG AAA compliant color contrasts with clear borders.
  • Instant Shortcuts: Press Alt + 3 for financial synonyms, Alt + 6 for inline AI explanations, and Ctrl to stop voice audio.
  • Curated Learning Curriculum: Beginner-friendly explanations of markets, institutional participants, and SEBI regulations.

What We Offer

  • Structured stock market curriculum with lesson takeaways and discussion forums.
  • Educational market news and weekly newsletter digests.
  • Interactive stock search with day's metrics and historical price tables.
  • Ask 360 AI Assistant for financial questions.
